## Double C Deer Processing

#### Contributor Agreements

1. Must Wash Hands When Returning to Work
2. No trash or cans in the disposal tubs
3.  Must Finish Current Task & Put Away Before Break
4. Deer Should Be In Order
5. Deer Shall Be Cut in **Date** Order
6. Equipment Shall Be Properly Cleaned After Use
    - Use Bleach if appropriate
    - Laundry included
7.  Must Take Appropriate Steps to Prevent Cross-Contamination
8. Tobacco Products Must Be Properly Disposed
    - Not in floor or disposal tubs
9. All meat shall go to the customer, unless specific arrangements are made between Management and Customer
10.  Mix Grind After First Run Before Running Again
11.  Deer are not considered READY for cut until form is in cutters book sleeve.
12.  Only Authorized Personnel in Cutting Room.
